## Overview

This repository showcases the work carried out during my **Research Internship at the Indian Institute of Information Technology, Allahabad (IIIT Allahabad)** under the **Cryptography and Network Security (CNS) Lab**.

During this internship, I worked on **IoT Security**, **Firmware Reverse Engineering**, and **Vulnerability Research**, focusing on the security assessment of embedded IP camera firmware. The research resulted in the responsible disclosure of security vulnerabilities that were officially assigned **two CVEs** and published by **CERT-In**.
